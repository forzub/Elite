# Equipment design schools (author 2026-11-10)

One base concept for all equipment (geometry only, serviceable units as separate parts, hit volumes, 4 LODs), but every
maker designs in the spirit of its colony and history. Similar modules of different makers must NOT look alike, must use
their own mounting / flange standard and may differ in envelope, so fitting a foreign unit is an adapter job.
Colony sources: Notes/lore/COLONIES/*.md (bloc ideology at the end of each file). Palettes are (Kd) colours; material
ids are `eq_<maker>_*` registered by each unit module (shared `eq_*` kernel materials stay available).

Rules of thumb
- The silhouette tells the maker at LOD2 already (shape language), the palette at LOD1, the interface and fasteners at LOD1-LOD0.
- Serviceable units stay readable as separate blocks in every school (the maintenance game needs them), even Bastion's
  (behind armoured hatches) and Zero Point's (sealed blocks with keyed sockets).
- Factions without own industry (Варпстанис, Ново-Фиваида, Сельва-Верде) do not make equipment; they run others' units
  (pirate field adapters, overheated ritual operation, bio-film pads) - proposal: docs/NON_INDUSTRIAL_FACTION_EQUIPMENT.md.
